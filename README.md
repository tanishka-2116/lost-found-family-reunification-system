# RakshaQR — Family Reunification System

**A privacy-conscious system designed to help identify, assist, and reunite people with their families during lost-person and emergency situations.**

## The Problem

When someone gets separated from their family in a crowded place, emergency, railway station, public event, or other unfamiliar environment, finding the right person quickly can become difficult.

Information about a missing or found person is often scattered between families, authorities, volunteers, and different locations. This can slow down identification and communication at the moment when time matters most.

## Our Approach

**RakshaQR** provides a simple way to connect a person with their trusted emergency information through a unique QR-based identity.

A user can create a safety profile, generate a personal QR code, and choose what information can be viewed when the QR is scanned.

The system is designed around a simple idea:

> **Help the right person get the right information at the right time — without exposing everything about them.**

## Key Features

- Personal safety profile
- Unique QR code generation
- QR scanning and identification
- Emergency contact information
- Medical information
- Lost / Found / Safe status
- One-tap emergency calling
- Privacy controls for shared information
- User dashboard
- Administrative controls
- Family and emergency-contact focused workflow

## How It Works

1. **Create Profile**  
   The user adds relevant identification and emergency information.

2. **Generate QR**  
   A unique QR code is generated for the user's profile.

3. **Scan When Needed**  
   An authorized or permitted person can scan the QR code.

4. **View Permitted Information**  
   Only the information intended to be shared is displayed.

5. **Contact the Right Person**  
   Emergency contacts or guardians can be reached when required.

## Vision

RakshaQR is being developed with the intention of going beyond a basic QR application.

Our goal is to build a practical system that can support faster identification and safer reunification in real-world situations while keeping privacy and human verification at the center of the process.

Further development will focus on improving the lost/found workflow, verification process, deployment readiness, and usability in crowded or emergency environments.
